About Home Shoppe

Your neighbourhood shop, run like family.

Tucked into 9th Main in HRBR Layout, Home Shoppe has been the go-to supermarket for households across Kalyan Nagar looking for daily groceries without the drive to a big-box store. From pantry staples and fresh produce to household essentials and personal care, we keep the shelves stocked with what families actually reach for every day.

Our team knows the neighbourhood and the regulars by name — because a complete family shop isn't just about what's on the shelf, it's about the people behind the counter.

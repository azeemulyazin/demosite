About Us

The Neighbourhood Store That Never Takes a Day Off

7 Days Super Market sits on 7th Cross, Nanjappa Garden in Babusapalya — a full-shelf grocery store built around one simple promise: keep it fresh, keep it fair.

We stock everything a Bengaluru kitchen runs through in a week — fruits and vegetables brought in each morning, dairy and bakery, rice and pulses, spices, frozen foods and household care. And because we are open all seven days, there is never a day you have to plan around us.
